About & reminders

This app saves entries only on this phone. No account is needed. Headache data stays on-device.

Daily slots: Morning (08:00), Midday (13:00), Evening (19:00).

Daily reminders

In the Android app, reminders can be scheduled on this phone at 08:00, 13:00, and 19:00 and open the correct check-in when tapped. In the browser/PWA version, exact background reminders are not guaranteed.
